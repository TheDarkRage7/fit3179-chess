Chapter 03

The first move matters

What can Australian players learn from opening choices online? Explore a historical sample of Lichess games, from popular opening families to the first move and reply. This sample describes past games, not current platform-wide trends.

How do chess games begin?

The Sicilian Defense appears in 13.0% of this sample, making it the most common individual opening family. Larger rectangles represent more games. Hover for full opening names, counts and percentages. The grey rectangle combines the smaller opening families.

Source: Chess Game Dataset on Kaggle, collected from Lichess . Based on 15,117 unique rated games from the 2013–2017 dataset after excluding game IDs with conflicting details. Variations are grouped by the opening name before the colon. The top 12 families are shown separately; all others are combined. This sample does not represent current popularity across all Lichess games.

Do opening preferences change with rating?

Among the six families shown, the Sicilian Defense rises from fourth in games averaging below 1200 to first in every higher rating group. Follow each line to compare preferences. Click a legend entry to highlight an opening.

Source: Chess Game Dataset on Kaggle, collected from Lichess . Uses the same cleaned historical sample of 15,117 rated games. Groups use the average of White and Black's Lichess ratings, which are separate from FIDE ratings. Rankings compare only the six families shown, not all openings. Differences between groups do not show players changing preferences over time.

White moves. How does Black reply?

Every opening begins with a choice and a response. Follow the paths from White's first move on the left to Black's reply on the right. Wider paths represent more games. Hover over a path for counts and percentages.

Reading the notation: e4 means a pawn moves to the e4 square; Nf6 means a knight moves to f6.

Source: Chess Game Dataset on Kaggle, collected from Lichess . Based on 15,111 unique rated games from the cleaned historical sample. Six games with fewer than two recorded moves were excluded. Less common first moves and replies are grouped as Other.

Chapter 04

On the world stage

International tournaments bring national teams together. Trace the Chess Olympiad's host cities, then explore how Australia performed against its starting seed in Budapest in 2024.

Where has the Chess Olympiad been hosted?

These cities hosted completed Chess Olympiads between 2000 and 2026. Each city has one equal-sized dot. Colour shows its latest hosting period. Hover over a dot to see all its hosting years.

Source: OlimpBase — Chess Olympiad history . The 2020 edition was cancelled. Markers use approximate city-centre coordinates, not exact tournament venues. Map boundaries: World Atlas / Natural Earth.

How did Australia perform at the 2024 Olympiad?

Australia's women's team finished 36th, ten places above its starting seed of 46th. The Open team finished 45th, eight places below its starting seed of 37th.

Read each line from left to right. Starting seed is the pre-tournament ranking based on player ratings; final position is the actual result. Lower numbers are better, so an upward line indicates a finish above the starting seed. The teams competed in separate events.

Source: Chess-Results, 2024 Open results and 2024 Women's results . Final standings after 11 rounds.
